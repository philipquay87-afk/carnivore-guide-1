Aajonus rules

The habits that make the food work.

No water for 30 minutes after meat

Water dilutes stomach acid and stalls the digestion of raw protein. Wait, then sip.

Always eat fat with protein

Lean raw meat alone stresses the kidneys. Raw butter, marrow or cream goes with every meat meal.

Sip milk, never gulp it

Raw milk drunk quickly floods the stomach. Small sips keep the enzymes working.

Pair honey with fat

Honey alone spikes energy; honey with raw butter or cream feeds you steadily.

Nothing above body temperature

Heat denatures protein and kills enzymes. If it was warmed past lukewarm, it is no longer primal.
